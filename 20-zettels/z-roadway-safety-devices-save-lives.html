Z - Roadway Safety Devices Save Lives

💡 Core Idea

Traffic signs, pavement markings, roadway lighting, delineation devices, and roadside barriers provide complementary functions — guiding road users, improving visibility, communicating hazards, and reducing the consequences of roadway departure. Treatments should be site-specific and warranted, not uniformly applied.

📖 Explanation

Traffic Signs and Pavement Markings

  • Communicate regulations, warnings, directions
  • Influence driver awareness, decision-making, compliance
  • Curves require particular attention — drivers need sufficient information and guidance
  • Clear, understandable signs reduce uncertainty

Roadway Lighting

  • Improves visibility during nighttime and adverse weather
  • Allows earlier detection of pedestrians, obstacles, hazards
  • Enhances hazard recognition and reaction time
  • Lit vs. unlit road environments show differences in injury-accident risk

Delineation Devices

  • Chevron alignment markers
  • Roadside delineators
  • Reflective pavement studs
  • Active road studs can complement or substitute continuous lighting in rural environments

Roadside Safety Devices

  • Guardrails, crash barriers, protective features
  • Prevent vehicles from striking hazardous objects
  • Reduce severity of roadway departure incidents
  • Essential where steep embankments, waterways, fixed objects exist

🔬 Evidence

  • Kučina et al. (2023): Curves important locations for safety treatments
  • Elvik (2009): Road-lighting effects using accident data; differences in injury-accident risk between lit and unlit roads
  • Active road studs research: Reflective/active delineation improves driver confidence and guidance at night
  • Bermudo & Rabacal: Philippine study — visible, appropriately placed, properly maintained traffic signs support road safety
  • Robielos & Lin (2022): Higher traffic sign comprehension among Filipino road users → safer driving behavior
  • Calderón Ramírez et al. (2023): Inadequate signage among most frequent deficiencies in Road Safety Audits
  • Bullough et al. (2013): Roadway lighting improvements enhance visual performance and nighttime safety
  • Plainis et al. (2020): Improved illumination enhances hazard recognition and reaction time
  • PIARC (2019): Roadside safety measures essential where hazards increase crash severity
  • AASHTO (2018): Recommends appropriate roadside protection measures
  • WHO (2023): Traffic control devices, visibility, protective infrastructure reduce injuries and fatalities

🎯 Key Principle

These features serve not only as protective infrastructure but also as important risk-mitigation measures within roadway design.

🎯 Application to This Study

  • Site-specific and warranted safety measures
  • May include:
    • Regulatory and warning signs
    • Pavement markings
    • Chevron alignment markers
    • Roadside delineators
    • Reflective pavement studs
    • Roadside barriers
    • Localized lighting
  • Continuous lighting or uniform installation of all devices throughout 850-m segment not automatically assumed
  • Treatments based on identified hazards, nighttime visibility requirements, geometric conditions, roadside risks, applicable standards
